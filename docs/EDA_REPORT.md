# 📊 Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) Report: PTB-XL ECG Dataset

The **PTB-XL Electrocardiography Dataset** is a large, publicly available clinical dataset comprising **21,837 clinical 10-second 12-lead ECG records** collected from **18,885 unique patients**.

---

## 📈 Dataset Overview & Summary Statistics

| Statistic | Value |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Total ECG Records** | 21,799 (validated records) |
| **Total Unique Patients** | 18,869 |
| **Number of Leads** | 12 Leads (I, II, III, aVR, aVL, aVF, V1, V2, V3, V4, V5, V6) |
| **Signal Duration** | 10.0 Seconds |
| **Sampling Rates Available** | 100 Hz (`records100`) and 500 Hz (`records500`) |
| **Number of Diagnostic Superclasses** | 5 Classes (`NORM`, `MI`, `STTC`, `CD`, `HYP`) |

---

## 🏷️ Diagnostic Superclass Distribution

In accordance with SCP-ECG diagnostic statements mapping (`scp_statements.csv`), records are mapped into 5 primary diagnostic superclasses:

1. **`NORM` (Normal ECG)**: 9,514 records (43.6%)
2. **`MI` (Myocardial Infarction)**: 5,469 records (25.1%)
3. **`STTC` (ST/T Change)**: 5,235 records (24.0%)
4. **`CD` (Conduction Disturbance)**: 4,898 records (22.5%)
5. **`HYP` (Hypertrophy)**: 2,649 records (12.2%)

> **Note**: PTB-XL is a multi-label dataset; single ECG recordings frequently exhibit multiple concurrent cardiac conditions (e.g., Myocardial Infarction combined with Conduction Disturbance).

---

## 👥 Patient Demographic Distribution

- **Age Distribution**: Ranges from 2 to 95 years, with a mean patient age of **59.8 years**.
- **Sex Distribution**: 52.1% Male, 47.9% Female.
- **Height & Weight**: Average height is **168.4 cm**, average weight is **74.6 kg**.

---

## 🔬 Benchmark Stratified Fold Splitting

To ensure standardized research benchmarking, PTB-XL provides an official 10-fold split (`strat_fold` column):
- **Training Folds (1 to 8)**: 17,418 records (80%)
- **Validation Fold (9)**: 2,183 records (10%)
- **Held-Out Test Fold (10)**: 2,198 records (10%)
